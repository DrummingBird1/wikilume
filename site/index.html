Wikipedia, made for reading.

A calm, modern layout with contents, themes, previews, bookmarks and multi-language search. Free. No account. No tracking.

Everything you need to read comfortably

Clean layout

Large title, reading time, a floating table of contents and the infobox in a side column.

Themes

Automatic, light, sepia and dark, with tables, templates and formulas adapted. Your font, size and width.

Previews and search

Hover cards for links and citations, suggestions as you type, random article, language switcher.

Multi-language search

Tick extra Wikipedias and compare their results side by side.

Bookmarks

Save articles, filter, export and import as a file. In the page and in the toolbar popup.

Timeline

Dated events picked out of the article text.

On-device summary

Written on your own device by Chrome's built-in AI, only after you agree to the one-time model download.

Right-to-left ready

Full Hebrew and RTL support, English or Hebrew interface, read-aloud, cover photo and lightbox.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads, no remote code. The only network traffic goes to Wikipedia itself. Settings and bookmarks stay in your browser. The only permission is "storage". Read the privacy policy
